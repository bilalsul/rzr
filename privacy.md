## Privacy Policy

RZV does not collect any information. There is no tracking and no analytics.

RZV uses the INTERNET permission to download repository archives (`.zip` files) requested by the user and to check for new versions on GitHub. No personal data is uploaded to any server. RZV also uses for temporary file access only when you are trying to add .zip file locally.

All the imported repositories are processed in the App Sandbox on your device and can be read offline. The app does not send your files, your reading history, or any other data to remote servers.

For any question, contact us at the [RZV support page](https://bilsul.com/support).
